Artistocat Cafe

111 N Liberty St, Harrisonburg VA, 22802 540-584-0020

Coffee • Tea • Vegan
  • A cat cafe tea house downtown that serves tea, espresso and snacks that can be enjoyed with takeout, dining inside while watching the adoptable kitties play in their seperate own room.
  • Offers wide selection of plant-based milk alternatives for your drink and vegan baked goods

Bella Gelato & Pastries

49A W Water St, Harrisonburg VA, 22801 540-217-5657

Coffee • Ice Cream • Vegan
  • Sister coffee shop to Bella Luna. They pride themselves on baked goods baked daily, locally roasted beans. Their handmade gelato is made on site with Mount Crawford Creamery dairy.
  • Offers plant milks for their espresso drinks as well as vegan-friendly gelato and sorbet flavors.

Pulp Cafe

135 S Main St, Harrisonburg VA, 22801 540-437-1922

Japanese • Asian • Vegan
  • Cafe located inside the Shenandoah Bicycle Co. on main street with smoothie drink and bowls to enjoy with Japanese goods.
  • Offers vegan-friendly acai bowls and smoothies with plant-based milk options. Additionally have vegan and vegetarian japanaese inspired food to enjoy as well.

Sugar & Bean Cafe

318 N Main St, Bridgewater VA, 22812 540-246-3030

Soup • Sandwich • Bakery •Thai • Vegan
  • Cafe further out in Bridgewater that has many soups, smoothies, espresso drinks and baked goods to try.
